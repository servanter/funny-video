## General Questions

### What is Funny Video?

Funny Video is a free online AI tool that transforms ordinary videos into Soul Painting style — a viral, cartoonish aesthetic popular on TikTok, Instagram, and Douyin. You upload a video, select a style, and the AI generates a transformed version in seconds.

### What is Soul Painting style?

Soul Painting (灵魂画手) is a deliberately simplified, hand-drawn cartoon aesthetic characterized by bold outlines, exaggerated expressions, flat colors, and intentionally imperfect lines. Originally a Chinese internet meme, it has become one of the most viral video aesthetics on global social media platforms because of its humor and shareability.

### Do I need any video editing skills to use Funny Video?

No. Funny Video is designed for everyone — including people with zero editing experience. The entire process is three steps: upload your video, select a style, and download the result. No technical knowledge required.

### Is Funny Video free to use?

Yes, Funny Video offers a free tier that lets you try all features. Free accounts have a daily usage quota. For heavier use, premium plans offer higher quotas and additional features.

### What platforms is the output compatible with?

Videos created with Funny Video are compatible with all major platforms including TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Douyin, Xiaohongshu, WeChat Moments, and Twitter/X.

## How to Use

### How do I get started?

1. Go to [Funny Video](https://www.funny-video.top)
2. Create a free account or log in with Google/GitHub
3. Upload your video on the homepage
4. Select the Soul Painting transformation style
5. Wait for processing (usually under 60 seconds)
6. Download your transformed video and share

### What video formats does Funny Video accept?

Funny Video accepts most common video formats including MP4, MOV, and AVI. For best results, use MP4 format with H.264 encoding.

### How long does video processing take?

Most videos are processed within 30–90 seconds, depending on video length and current server load. Longer videos may take a few minutes.

### Is there a maximum video length or file size?

Free accounts can upload videos up to 60 seconds in length and 100MB in file size. Premium accounts have higher limits. For social media content, videos under 60 seconds tend to perform best anyway.

### Can I process multiple videos at once?

Currently, videos are processed one at a time. You can queue your next video while the current one is processing.

### What makes a good source video for Soul Painting transformation?

The best source videos have:
- Clear subjects (faces, objects, people)
- Good lighting (natural light is ideal)
- Moderate pacing (not extremely fast motion)
- Expressive content (reactions, pets, everyday moments)

Very dark footage, extremely fast camera movement, or very low-resolution video may produce less consistent results.

## Troubleshooting

### My video upload failed. What should I do?

First, check that your video file meets the format and size requirements (MP4/MOV/AVI, under 100MB for free accounts). If the issue persists, try refreshing the page and uploading again. If problems continue, contact us at zhy19890221@gmail.com.

### The transformation doesn't look the way I expected. Can I redo it?

Yes. You can re-upload your video and try the transformation again. If a particular video consistently produces poor results, try a different clip with better lighting and clearer subjects.

### I downloaded my video but the quality seems lower than expected.

Style transformation changes the visual aesthetic, which may affect how sharpness is perceived. Ensure your source video is at least 720p for best results. Very low-resolution source footage will produce lower-quality output regardless of the transformation applied.

### I can't log in to my account.

Try resetting your password via the login page. If you signed up using Google or GitHub login, make sure you're using the same method you originally used. For persistent login issues, contact support at zhy19890221@gmail.com.

### The processing is taking much longer than usual.

Occasional delays can occur during peak usage periods. If your video hasn't processed within 5 minutes, try refreshing the page. Your upload should still be available. If the issue persists, try uploading again or contact support.
