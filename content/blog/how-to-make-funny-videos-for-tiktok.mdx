Funny videos tick all of these boxes. When something makes you laugh, you watch it again. You send it to your friends. You save it to show people later. All of those actions signal to TikTok that your content is valuable, which means the algorithm pushes it to more people.

## Building a Consistent TikTok Presence

One video, no matter how funny, rarely leads to sustained growth. Here are some principles to build on your initial success:

**Post consistently** — aim for 3–5 posts per week when starting out. TikTok's algorithm rewards consistency.

**Engage with comments** — reply to comments on your videos, especially in the first hour after posting. This signals to the algorithm that your content is generating conversation.

**Use trending sounds and hashtags** — TikTok actively promotes content that uses trending audio. Check the Discover tab regularly for what's popular.

**Experiment with formats** — don't do the same thing every time. Try different content types and see what resonates with your audience.

**Collaborate with others** — duets and stitches with other creators expose your content to their audience.
